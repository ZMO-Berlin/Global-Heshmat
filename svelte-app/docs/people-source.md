# People source and integration

The sole source for the People section is `Akteurs_draft.docx`, supplied by the project owner on 5 October 2026. Its SHA-256 is recorded in `src/lib/data/people.json`. Paragraph indices are zero-based Word body paragraphs, including empty paragraphs. The file contains 31 profiles in eight source groups, two shared contextual passages and two footnotes. Shant (Chant) Avetisyan's repeated “see above” entry becomes one profile in two groups.

The owner explicitly requested: omit draft notes, preserve biography wording. Removed material consists of list markers, collaborator/contact asterisks, the draft title and working introduction, the `XXX` placeholder, the photo reminder, `+ aunt in Heeze`, the editorial aside in the peers heading, the German aside in Sabry Ragheb's paragraph and the archival question in the Steppat footnote. Whitespace is normalized. Quotation annotations (`[Heshmat, SH]`, `[…]`), spelling, dates, first-person recollections and both substantive footnotes remain as supplied. No biography, portrait, nationality or date has been supplemented from another source.

Shared family passages remain intact. The two source paragraphs that split Ronald Pritchard's opening sentence are joined with a space. Gamal as-Sigini and Nadine Nour Eddine link to the Emad Abu Ghazi passage identified by their source's “see above.” Shared passages about the portraitists and Warsaw delegation appear with the relevant names and links, preserving their context. The source's external citation is rendered as a text link.

People have independent static routes and a separate data model, without map coordinates or artwork location statuses. The shared search searches names, full passages, notes and shared context. Choosing People exposes source-group and place-mentioned facets; places are literal mentions in the passage or its source heading, not inferred residences or nationalities. Country and artwork status facets apply to collection records. Search results in the collection show People separately from artwork albums and exports.

Explicit source mentions are connected to existing catalogue records: Ain Shams residence/museum, the Haude residence, Susteren mosaics, Heliopolis Sheraton, Oriental Weavers, BTM sites and Kafraoui Park, The Dawn of Egypt and its former site, and Cairo International Stadium. These links are reciprocal and supply no additional biography text. Catalogue wording and dates may differ from the draft; neither source is silently reconciled with the other.

Profiles are prerendered, included in the sitemap and cached for offline return after a visit. The People index is part of the offline shell. The existing fieldbook and collection exports continue to describe artwork/residence records.
